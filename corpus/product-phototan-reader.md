The photoTAN reader is the offline alternative to the pushTAN app for customers who do not use a smartphone.

## How it works

The reader is a small dedicated device with a camera. During a transaction, online banking displays a colored mosaic graphic; the reader scans it and shows the transaction details plus a 7-digit TAN on its own display. The TAN is entered in the browser to confirm.

## Ordering and activation

The reader costs 24.90 euros one-time and ships within 5 business days. Activation uses the same posted activation letter flow as pushTAN. A customer can have either pushTAN or photoTAN active, not both; switching methods re-issues the activation letter.

## Battery and lifetime

Two AAA batteries last roughly two years. The device has no network connection and receives no updates — a defective reader is simply replaced, and readers older than 8 years are proactively exchanged for current hardware at no cost.
