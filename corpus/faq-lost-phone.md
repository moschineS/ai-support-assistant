## Immediate steps

1. Block the pushTAN registration right away: call the 24/7 blocking hotline 116 116 (from abroad: +49 30 4050 4050) or use "Block access" on the public website. Blocking takes effect immediately.
2. Online banking stays blocked until a new device is registered — payments via cards continue to work.

## Registering a new phone

Install the Aventra pushTAN app on the new device and choose "Move registration". If you can still receive the confirmation letter code by post, re-activation takes 3 to 5 business days. Branch customers can alternatively re-activate in person with an ID document in about 10 minutes.

## What a thief can and cannot do

Without your app password and device unlock, the pushTAN app does not display or approve anything. Login to online banking additionally requires your banking password. Report any suspicious approval prompt you did not trigger to the fraud team immediately.
