## Self-service change

Address, email, and phone number can be changed in online banking under Profile, then Personal data. Address changes require a pushTAN confirmation. The new address is effective immediately for all correspondence.

## When documents are required

A move abroad, a name change, or a correction of the date of birth cannot be done self-service. These require a certified document (registration certificate, marriage certificate, or ID document) submitted through the document upload in the app or in a branch. Processing takes up to 5 business days.

## Why keeping the address current matters

Activation letters for pushTAN re-registration and replacement PINs are sent exclusively by post to the registered address and cannot be redirected. An outdated address is the most common cause of a delayed account recovery.
