AventraCard Classic is the standard credit card with monthly full settlement.

## Key facts

- Card scheme: internationally accepted; contactless and mobile wallet support.
- Billing: all transactions of a month are collected and debited in full from the reference account on the 3rd of the following month. No revolving credit, no interest.
- Fee: 2.90 euros per month; included at no charge in AventraGiro Plus.
- Limit: assigned individually after a credit check; visible in the app under Cards, then Limit. Temporary limit raises for travel can be requested in the app up to twice a year.

## Security features

Every online payment requires a pushTAN approval (3-D Secure). Regional blocking lets you disable card regions (for example, all non-European transactions) in the app. Declined point-of-sale payments with reference CP-0031 relate to a known incident, not to the card's limit.
