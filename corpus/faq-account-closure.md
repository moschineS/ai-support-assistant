## How to close

An account closure request can be submitted in writing (form in online banking under Service, then Account closure) or in a branch. The request must name a reference account for the remaining balance. Closure is free of charge and effective within 10 business days; by law no notice period applies to consumer current accounts.

## What blocks a closure

Open card transactions that have not settled, an active overdraft in use, unsettled securities trades in a linked AventraDepot, or a negative balance. Support can tell you exactly which item is blocking and when it clears.

## After closure

Cards and access are deactivated on the closure date. Standing orders and direct debit mandates end; the bank does not forward incoming payments after closure — senders receive them back with the note "account closed". Statements remain downloadable for 90 days after closure; after that they are available on request for the legal retention period.
