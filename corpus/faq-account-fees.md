## Monthly account fee

AventraGiro Basic costs 4.90 euros per month; AventraGiro Plus costs 9.90 euros per month. The fee is debited on the first business day of the following month and appears as "Kontofuehrung" on the statement. Customers under 27 and customers with a monthly salary inflow above 3,000 euros on Plus pay no account fee.

## Common additional fees

- Paper statement by post: 1.50 euros per statement (electronic statements are free).
- Replacement card: 8 euros, free when the card was faulty or fraud-affected.
- Cash withdrawal at third-party ATMs: free at partner network machines, otherwise the operator surcharge shown at the machine applies.

## Disputed or unexpected fees

If a fee looks wrong, do not wait for the statement cycle: support can trace every fee booking to its trigger. Goodwill refunds follow the fee refund procedure and are decided within 5 business days.
