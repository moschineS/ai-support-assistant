## Standard SEPA transfer

Transfers submitted on a business day before the 15:30 cut-off are credited to the recipient bank on the next business day. Submissions after cut-off, on weekends, or on TARGET holidays are processed the following business day.

## SEPA instant transfer

Instant transfers are credited within 10 seconds, around the clock, every day of the year, up to 100,000 euros per transfer. Both the sending and receiving bank must support the instant scheme; if the recipient bank does not, the order is automatically executed as a standard transfer and you are informed before confirming.

## When a transfer seems missing

Check the processing status under Payments, then Order history. A rejected instant transfer shows error code TR-4400 with a reason text. Standard transfers cannot be recalled once executed; a recall request to the recipient bank can be filed through support and takes up to 10 business days for an answer.
