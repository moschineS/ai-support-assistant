## When it triggers

Customer data must be re-verified on a risk-based cycle (standard risk: every 5 years; elevated risk: every 2 years; high risk: yearly) and event-driven after an expired ID document, a move abroad, or unusual account activity flagged by monitoring.

## Process for agents

1. The customer receives a secure-mailbox message and, after 30 days without response, a letter.
2. Re-verification runs self-service in the app (document scan plus liveness check) in about 10 minutes, or via video identification, or in a branch.
3. Agents can see the deadline and current status in the customer record under Compliance, then KYC status. Agents cannot extend deadlines.

## Escalation and consequences

If re-verification is not completed within 90 days of the first notice, outgoing payments are restricted (statutory obligation); after 180 days the account is blocked pending verification. Restrictions lift automatically within one hour of successful verification. Hardship cases (hospital stay, long absence) go to the compliance duty desk — never promise an exception, only the review.
