Instant transfers move money to any participating European bank within 10 seconds, at any time.

## Conditions

Available to all account holders at no extra charge on AventraGiro Plus; on Basic each instant transfer costs 0.49 euros. The per-transfer maximum is 100,000 euros. Instant transfers are final — they cannot be recalled, so the confirmation screen shows the verified recipient name when the recipient bank supports name matching.

## Recipient name check

Before execution, the recipient bank returns how well the entered name matches the account holder: match, close match (the actual name is shown for confirmation), or no match. A no-match warning should be taken seriously; fraud cases almost always show a no-match.

## Failures

If the recipient bank does not participate in the instant scheme, the app offers standard execution instead. Technical rejections show error code TR-4400 with a reason; money never leaves the account on a rejection — the amount is only reserved and the reservation clears within minutes.
