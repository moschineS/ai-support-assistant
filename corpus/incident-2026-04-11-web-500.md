## Summary

On 2026-04-11 between 09:15 and 10:50, web banking intermittently returned the generic error page ("Es ist ein technischer Fehler aufgetreten", reference **IB-0500**) on roughly every fifth request. The mobile app was degraded but mostly usable. No data was lost; submitted transfers either executed completely or not at all.

## Status

Resolved same day. A database connection pool exhausted during the morning batch window; pool sizing and batch scheduling were changed on 2026-04-14 to prevent recurrence.

## Verifying whether a transfer went through

Customers who submitted a transfer during the window and saw IB-0500 should check Payments, then Order history. An order listed there with status "executed" ran exactly once — duplicates were prevented by the idempotency check. If the order is absent, it was never accepted and must be re-submitted.
