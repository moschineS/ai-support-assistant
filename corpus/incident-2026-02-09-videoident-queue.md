## Summary

Between 2026-02-09 and 2026-02-16, waiting times for video identification (new customers and KYC re-verification) reached up to 90 minutes during evenings; many sessions were abandoned. Support reference: **KY-1900**. Cause: a marketing campaign tripled onboarding volume while the identification provider was already at seasonal capacity.

## Status

Resolved 2026-02-16 by adding a second identification provider and a callback option (customers book a slot instead of waiting in the live queue).

## Handling stuck onboardings

Applications abandoned mid-identification stay valid for 30 days and resume where they stopped — nothing must be re-entered. The booking link is on the application status page. Customers with an urgent account need (e.g. salary account switch with a deadline) can complete identification in any branch without an appointment; branch identification has priority handling since this incident.
