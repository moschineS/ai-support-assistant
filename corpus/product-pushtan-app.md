The pushTAN app is the approval instrument for online banking: logins, payments, and profile changes are confirmed on the registered phone.

## Registration

Registration requires the activation letter (sent by post) and takes about 5 minutes: install the app, scan the letter's QR code, set an app password, and confirm. One registration is bound to one device; a phone change moves the registration with a new letter or in a branch.

## Daily use

Approval prompts appear as push notifications and under "Pending approvals" inside the app. Each prompt shows what is being approved (login, transfer with amount and recipient, or profile change) — read it before approving. Prompts expire after 5 minutes.

## Troubleshooting

Delayed or missing prompts are usually notification-delivery problems; incident EB-1042 tracks the known delay pattern. The app refuses to run on rooted or jailbroken devices and on OS versions older than iOS 15 or Android 10. Time drift on the phone larger than 2 minutes causes approval failures — enable automatic time.
