## Creating a standing order

In online banking or the mobile app choose Payments, then Standing orders, then New. Define recipient, amount, frequency (weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly) and the first execution date. Confirmation requires a pushTAN approval. The first execution must be at least one business day in the future.

## Changing or pausing

Existing orders can be edited or paused until 22:00 on the day before the next execution. Changes also require pushTAN confirmation. A paused order keeps its schedule and resumes automatically when you unpause it.

## Failed executions

If the account lacks funds at 07:00 on the execution day, the order is retried once at 12:00. After a second failure the execution is skipped and you receive a notification; the order itself stays active for the next cycle. Skipped executions are never caught up automatically.
