## Why a card gets blocked

The most common causes are three consecutive wrong PIN entries, a fraud-prevention block after unusual transactions, an expired card that was not activated, or a block the customer set themselves in the mobile app.

## Unblocking

- **Wrong PIN three times:** the card unblocks itself at any Aventra ATM by performing a balance inquiry with the correct PIN. It cannot be unblocked by phone.
- **Fraud-prevention block:** support can lift it after confirming the flagged transactions with you. Have the transaction list ready.
- **Self-set block in the app:** toggle it off yourself under Cards, then Security. The unblock is effective within two minutes.

## If the card was blocked after suspected fraud

A card blocked because of confirmed third-party fraud is never unblocked; a replacement card ships automatically within 5 business days and the fraud report procedure applies. Point-of-sale declines that show error code CP-0031 are a known processing incident, not a fraud block — check the incident log first.
