## What agents may refund directly

First-line agents can refund, once per customer per rolling 12 months, without approval: one paper statement fee, one third-party ATM surcharge complaint (as goodwill, since the surcharge is the operator's), and one replacement card fee when the customer plausibly reports a faulty card. Direct refunds are booked immediately and logged with reason code.

## What requires the fee desk

Account fee disputes (e.g. "the waiver condition was met"), interest calculations, repeated refund requests, and any amount above 25 euros. The fee desk answers within 5 business days. Agents check the objective condition first — for the Plus fee waiver, the 3,000 euro salary inflow is visible in the fee details of the statement — and attach it to the case.

## What is never refunded as goodwill

Fees resulting from a legally required action (e.g. returned direct debit due to insufficient funds after the low-balance notification was delivered) and third-party fees the bank never received. Explain the cause and, where a product change would avoid the fee in future, offer the product change instead.
