AventraGiro Plus is the premium current account for customers who want cards and travel benefits bundled in.

## Included

Everything from AventraGiro Basic, plus an AventraCard Classic credit card at no extra charge, free cash withdrawals worldwide, and two partner cards for family members. Instant transfer fees are included.

## Conditions

9.90 euros per month; waived in any month with a salary inflow of at least 3,000 euros. The included AventraCard Classic follows the standard credit assessment.

## Upgrade and downgrade

Switching between Basic and Plus is possible monthly in online banking under Products, effective the first of the following month. Downgrading to Basic ends the included credit card unless it is continued as a paid stand-alone card; support confirms the future card fee before the downgrade is executed.
