## Requirements

Aventra Mobile requires iOS 16 or later, or Android 11 or later, and an active online banking contract. The pushTAN app is separate and must be installed alongside it for approvals.

## Activation steps

1. Install "Aventra Mobile" from the official app store.
2. Sign in with your customer number and online banking password.
3. Approve the device binding in the pushTAN app.
4. Choose an app-specific unlock method (PIN, fingerprint, or face unlock).

The device binding limits the app to three active devices; a fourth activation deactivates the oldest.

## Known issues

On Android beta OS versions the app may crash on startup; crashes on Android 15 beta builds are tracked as incident MB-1103. Reinstalling does not lose data — all data lives server-side and the device binding can be renewed at any time.
