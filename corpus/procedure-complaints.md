## What counts as a complaint

Any expression of dissatisfaction with a product, service, or handling — regardless of channel or wording. Agents register it in the complaint tool; "the customer was just venting" is not a reason to skip registration.

## Deadlines

Acknowledgement to the customer within 2 business days. Substantive answer within 15 business days; if that is impossible, an interim notice with the reason and expected date. These deadlines are supervisory requirements, not internal targets.

## Escalation ladder

1. First-line resolution by the receiving agent where possible.
2. Complaint management team for anything involving money, deadlines, or repeated contact.
3. The bank's ombudsman information is included in every final answer: customers can turn to the consumer arbitration board or the competent supervisory authority at no cost. Never discourage a customer from the arbitration route — the final answer must name it explicitly.

## Tone rules

Acknowledge the inconvenience concretely, state facts without blaming other departments, and give one clear next step with a date.
