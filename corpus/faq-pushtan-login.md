Online banking sign-in is a two-step process: password plus a pushTAN confirmation on your registered phone.

## How login works

Enter your customer number and password at aventra-bank.example/login. Within a few seconds the Aventra pushTAN app on your registered device shows a confirmation prompt with the login location and time. Approve it and the browser session opens automatically.

## If no pushTAN prompt arrives

1. Pull down to refresh inside the pushTAN app; prompts can take up to 30 seconds during peak hours.
2. Check that notifications are enabled for the app and that the phone has a network connection.
3. In the app, open "Pending approvals" — every waiting prompt is listed there even when the push notification was not delivered.
4. If prompts still do not arrive, check the service status page. Delivery delays are tracked under error code EB-1042.

## Related

Password forgotten: see the password reset FAQ. New phone: the pushTAN registration must be moved with your activation letter.
