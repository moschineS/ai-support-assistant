AventraSparen Flex is the daily-access savings account linked to the current account.

## Key facts

- Variable interest, credited quarterly; the current rate is always shown in the app under Products, then Savings.
- No minimum balance, no fees, no notice period: transfers between Flex and the current account are instant and unlimited.
- Deposits are covered by the statutory deposit guarantee scheme up to 100,000 euros per customer.

## Automatic saving

Two automations are available: a monthly savings plan (fixed amount on a fixed day) and round-up saving, which rounds every card payment up to the next full euro and moves the difference to Flex. Both can be paused any time.

## Limits

Flex is a savings account, not a payment account: outgoing transfers can only go to the linked Aventra current account, never to third parties. This is a security property — even a compromised online banking session cannot move savings directly to an external account.
