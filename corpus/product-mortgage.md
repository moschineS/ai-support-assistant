AventraHome finances owner-occupied residential property with fixed-rate periods of 5 to 30 years.

## Basics

Financing up to 90 percent of the purchase price; the rate depends on fixed-rate period, loan-to-value, and repayment rate. An initial repayment rate of at least 2 percent is standard. Unscheduled repayments of up to 5 percent of the original loan per year are included free.

## Application process

1. Online pre-check (10 minutes, no documents) gives a non-binding rate indication.
2. A financing appointment (video or branch) reviews income and property documents.
3. The binding offer is valid for 14 days; after signing, payout follows the notary schedule.

Typical time from appointment to binding offer: 5 to 10 business days, driven mostly by document completeness.

## Support topics

Rate adjustment at fixed-period end (Anschlussfinanzierung) starts up to 36 months in advance. Payment difficulties: contact the financing team early — deferral and restructuring options exist and are handled case by case, before the dunning process ever starts.
