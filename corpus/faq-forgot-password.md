## Self-service reset

On the login page choose "Forgotten password". You need your customer number, date of birth, and an active pushTAN registration. After identity confirmation via pushTAN you set a new password immediately; the old one is invalidated at the same moment.

## When self-service is not possible

If your pushTAN registration is also lost (for example after a phone change), the reset requires a re-activation letter sent by post to your registered address. The letter arrives within 3 to 5 business days and contains a one-time activation code valid for 30 days.

## Password rules

Passwords must be 10 to 64 characters and contain at least one digit and one special character. The five most recent passwords cannot be reused. After five failed attempts access is locked for 30 minutes; after ten failed attempts the account is blocked and support must unblock it after identity verification.
