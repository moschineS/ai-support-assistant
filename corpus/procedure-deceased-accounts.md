## First contact

Handle with explicit condolence and without time pressure. Record the notifying person's name, relationship, and contact data. Request a death certificate (upload, post, or branch); no account action is taken on a phone call alone.

## What happens on notification

The account converts to an estate account: cards and online banking access of the deceased are deactivated, standing orders continue (rent, utilities — they are often needed), and direct debits continue unless the estate representative objects. Powers of attorney expire only if they were limited to the lifetime; a transmortal power of attorney remains valid and its holder can dispose of the account with certificate and ID.

## Who can access the estate

Heirs with a certificate of inheritance (Erbschein) or a notarized will with opening protocol; holders of a transmortal power of attorney; and, for funeral costs, the bank may pay invoices directly to the funeral home from the account against the invoice, before an Erbschein exists. Individual balances up to 5,000 euros can be released to close relatives against a declaration of assumption of costs — fee desk approval required.
