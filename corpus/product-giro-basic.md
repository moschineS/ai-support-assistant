AventraGiro Basic is the entry current account for everyday banking.

## Included

Online and mobile banking, a debit card, SEPA transfers including instant transfers, standing orders, and electronic statements. Cash withdrawals are free at all Aventra and partner network ATMs.

## Conditions

4.90 euros per month, waived for customers under 27. No minimum inflow. An overdraft facility can be added after six months of account history, subject to a credit check.

## Not included

Credit cards are a separate product (AventraCard Classic or Gold). Paper statements cost 1.50 euros each. Foreign-currency payments outside the SEPA area carry the standard price-list charges.

## Switching service

The digital account switching service transfers standing orders, direct debit mandates, and salary notifications from the old bank within 10 business days and is free of charge.
