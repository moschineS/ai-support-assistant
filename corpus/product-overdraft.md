The overdraft facility lets the current account go negative up to an agreed limit.

## Conditions

Available after six months of account history or immediately with a verified salary inflow. The limit is typically up to three net monthly salaries. Interest accrues daily only on the used amount at the rate published in the price list; there is no fee for having the unused facility.

## Applying and changing

Application, limit changes, and cancellation are self-service in online banking under Products, then Overdraft, with a pushTAN confirmation and, for raises, a fresh credit check. Reductions and cancellation are effective immediately; a raise is effective after approval, usually within 1 business day.

## When the account exceeds the limit

Payments that would exceed the agreed limit are declined. Direct debits that would breach the limit are returned unpaid, which can trigger third-party return fees — the app's low-balance push notification exists to catch this before it happens. An account over its limit for more than 30 days triggers the dunning process and a mandatory advisory contact.
