## Before filing a dispute

Check the merchant descriptor first: many "unknown" bookings are legitimate purchases under a parent company name. The mobile app shows the merchant's clear name and logo for most transactions under Transactions, then Details.

## How to file

A dispute can be filed in the app (Transactions, then Details, then Dispute) or through support. Required: the transaction date and amount, and the reason category — unauthorized transaction, wrong amount, goods not received, or duplicate booking. Filing is possible up to 8 weeks after the booking date for direct debits and up to 120 days for card payments.

## What happens next

Unauthorized card transactions are provisionally re-credited within 1 business day while the chargeback runs. Merchant disputes (goods not received, wrong amount) follow the chargeback procedure with the card scheme and typically resolve within 30 to 45 days. You are informed at every status change; the provisional credit is only reversed if the chargeback is lost.
