## Intake

Disputes arrive via the app dispute flow or support. Verify: transaction identified, reason category chosen (unauthorized, wrong amount, goods not received, duplicate), and — for merchant disputes — that the customer attempted to resolve with the merchant first (scheme requirement, except for unauthorized transactions).

## Deadlines (hard)

- Unauthorized card transaction: provisional re-credit within 1 business day of the claim; scheme filing within 13 months of booking.
- Merchant disputes: filing within 120 days of the booking (goods not received: 120 days from the promised delivery date).
- Direct debit returns: 8 weeks no-questions-asked; 13 months for unauthorized mandates.

## Handling rules for agents

Never promise an outcome, only the timeline: scheme responses typically take 30 to 45 days. The provisional credit is only reversed on a lost chargeback and the customer is informed in writing beforehand. Suspected fraud (not merchant conflict) additionally triggers the fraud report procedure and card replacement — the two procedures run in parallel, not either-or.
