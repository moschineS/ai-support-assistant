## Channels

- **Phone:** 0800 220 4400, Monday to Friday 07:00 to 20:00, Saturday 09:00 to 14:00.
- **Card and access blocking:** 116 116, around the clock (from abroad +49 30 4050 4050).
- **Secure mailbox:** in online banking under Service, then Messages — answered within 1 business day.
- **Branch appointments:** bookable in the app; walk-ins served as capacity allows.

## Which channel for what

Fraud suspicion and blocking: always phone, immediately. Document submissions: secure mailbox or app upload, never email. Complaints: any channel — they are routed to the complaint procedure with its own deadlines. Ordinary product questions are fastest through the secure mailbox outside phone hours.

## Identification on the phone

Phone support verifies identity with customer number plus two security questions, or a pushTAN app confirmation. Support never asks for your full password or a TAN code — treat any such request as a phishing attempt and report it.
