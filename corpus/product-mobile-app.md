Aventra Mobile is the full banking client for iOS and Android: accounts, cards, payments, depot, and document mailbox in one app.

## Feature overview

Account and card overview with real-time balances, SEPA and instant transfers, standing orders, card controls (block, regional block, limit view), photo transfer (scan an invoice to prefill a transfer), the secure document mailbox, and depot positions with order entry. Push notifications can be enabled per event type: incoming payments, card transactions, low balance, and security events.

## Devices and sessions

Up to three bound devices per customer. Sessions end automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity; a shorter timeout can be set under Profile, then Security. Screenshots are blocked on Android inside payment screens by design.

## Data and privacy

The app stores no transaction data on the device beyond the current session cache, which is wiped at logout. Biometric unlock uses the phone's secure enclave; the bank never receives biometric data.
