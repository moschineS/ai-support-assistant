AventraCard Gold extends the Classic card with travel benefits and higher limits.

## Key facts

- Fee: 8.90 euros per month, no waiver.
- Billing identical to Classic: full monthly settlement on the 3rd, no revolving credit.
- Higher standard limit tier and priority limit-raise handling.

## Included insurance

Travel cancellation insurance up to 5,000 euros per trip, international travel health insurance for trips up to 56 days, and rental car collision coverage — each valid when at least half of the trip price was paid with the card. Claims are handled by the insurance partner named in the policy documents in online banking under Documents, then Insurance.

## Eligibility

Gold requires 12 months of account history or a verified net income above 2,500 euros per month. An existing Classic card is upgraded in place: same card number, new benefits effective from the next billing cycle.
