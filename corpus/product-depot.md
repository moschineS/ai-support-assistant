AventraDepot is the brokerage account for shares, ETFs, and funds, managed inside online banking and the app.

## Conditions

No custody fee. Order commission: 0.25 percent of the order volume, minimum 4.90 euros, maximum 49 euros per order. ETF savings plans from 25 euros per month are free of order commission. Trading venues: German exchanges and direct trading partners.

## Orders and settlement

Orders can be placed with limits valid up to 90 days. Executions are confirmed by push notification and settlement note in the document mailbox; the cash side settles against the current account two business days after execution. Delayed order confirmations were the subject of incident DP-3302 — the order status page, not the push notification, is authoritative.

## Important limitations

AventraDepot executes orders without investment advice (execution-only). The legally required cost disclosure appears before every order confirmation. Complex products require completing the knowledge and experience questionnaire once before the first order.
